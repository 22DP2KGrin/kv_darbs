Present Simple

📘 Usage:

  • Regular actions or routines:
    She goes to work every day.
    I drink coffee in the morning.
  • General facts or truths:
    Water boils at 100°C.
    The sun rises in the east.
  • Schedules and timetables:
    The train leaves at 7 pm.
    My class starts at 9.

🔹 Affirmative Form:

Subject Verb (base form)
I / You / We / They play, work, eat (no change)
He / She / It plays, works, eats (add -s)

Examples:
I work in a bank.
He works in a hospital.

🔹 Spelling Rules for -s:

  • Most verbs: add -s → play → plays
  • Ends in -ch, -sh, -x, -ss, -o: add -es → watch → watches
  • Consonant + -y: change -y to -ies → study → studies
  • Vowel + -y: just add -s → play → plays

🔹 Negative Form:

Subject Auxiliary Verb (base form)
I / You / We / They do not (don't) like, go, read
He / She / It does not (doesn't) like, go, read

Examples:
I don't like milk.
She doesn't play tennis.

🔹 Question Form:

Auxiliary Subject Verb (base form)
Do you/we/they eat?
Does he/she/it eat?

Examples:
Do you like music?
Does he speak English?
